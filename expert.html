TIER A

Tier A · Star Contract Expert

Our long-term partners. You are first in line for high-value members, repeat guests and longer, tailored experiences. Income goals are clearer and overall earnings sit well above Tier B/C.

What CBODY provides

Priority access to high-value clients

High-spending members, repeat guests and long bookings are matched to you first where suitable.

Reference income targets

Clear reference ranges for monthly earnings and quarterly bonuses for meeting agreed goals.

Personal brand building

We help refine your cover photos, copywriting and video scripts to match a premium Expert image.

Prime exposure

Featured placements on the homepage, city pages and curated collections targeting your ideal guests.

Expectations & requirements

  • Content collaboration: creating content is required. CBODY provides scripts, references and editing support.
  • Stable availability: maintain reasonable working hours and avoid no-shows or last-minute cancellations.
  • Platform exclusivity: you may not appear on other similar on-site service platforms (except CBODY-approved third-party partners or venues).

How do I qualify for Tier A?

  • Promotion from Tier B with stable performance, strong reviews and high-quality content output.
  • Existing personal brand, social following or reputation — invited directly by CBODY.
  • Strong internal recommendations from trusted partners or venues.
TIER B

Tier B · Creator Partner Expert

Use content to attract better guests and higher rates. CBODY supports you with ideas, reference accounts and light editing — this tier is the main path to becoming a Star Contract Expert.

Benefits & support

  • Better guests, better position

    Higher visibility in search and recommendation sections means more suitable, higher-quality guests.

  • Content guidance

    We offer content themes, reference accounts and suggestions to help you film in a way guests understand and like.

  • Extra rewards

    Strong content performance may unlock bonuses, extra exposure or special campaign invitations.

Expectations & requirements

  • Content output: we recommend 2–3 pieces per week. At least one main social account should be registered with CBODY.
  • Service quality: maintain basic online hours and consistent service. Poor ratings over time may lead to downgrade.
  • Platform exclusivity: you may not appear on competing on-site platforms (except CBODY-approved partners or venues).

Promotion path

By keeping stable hours, strong ratings and consistent, high-quality content, Creator Partners can be invited to move up to Tier A and access higher ticket sizes and more predictable earnings.

TIER C

Tier C · Quality Part-time Expert

The entry level for joining CBODY. Ideal if you already have a main job and want an additional, flexible income stream. There is no income guarantee, but you receive baseline platform traffic. Accounts that rarely come online or never take bookings will be removed.

Basic requirements

Verified identity, accurate profile, clear photos and basic standards of courtesy and communication.

Promotion support

You should register at least one social account and be willing to post content prepared or adapted by CBODY at a light, reasonable frequency.

Online time & bookings

When you switch yourself online, you are expected to respond and accept bookings where reasonable. Long-term inactivity or very few orders will result in cooperation ending.

Exclusivity

You may not be listed on competing on-site platforms at the same time (CBODY-approved third-party venues are an exception). Working in offline shops or other industries is fine.

Key collaboration rules

1 Promotion is a basic requirement, not optional

Supporting CBODY’s promotion through your own accounts — by posting or re-posting agreed content — is a basic condition for joining the platform, not an add-on.

2 Exclusivity helps us focus resources on committed Experts

Experts may not appear on competing on-site service platforms (except for CBODY-approved third-party partners or venues). Exclusivity allows us to concentrate high-quality traffic and opportunities on Experts who are fully committed to CBODY.

3 Inactive or non-cooperative accounts will be removed

CBODY chooses to work with Experts who are serious and consistent. Accounts with very low online time, almost no orders, or repeated refusal to cooperate on basic optimizations will have their cooperation ended.
