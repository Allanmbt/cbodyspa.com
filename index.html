Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our outcall and home massage services.

What is Outcall Massage?
Outcall massage means the therapist comes to you! Instead of going to a spa, you can book a professional massage therapist to visit your home, hotel room, or condo. It offers maximum convenience and privacy.
Is CBODY safe?
Absolutely. Safety is our priority. All experts on CBODY are verified independent providers. We offer features like real-time chat, secure user accounts, and a transparent review system to ensure a safe experience for everyone.
How do I book a mobile massage?
Simply download the CBODY app, browse verified experts in your area (Bangkok, Pattaya, etc.), check their reviews and services, and click book. You can chat with them to confirm details before the appointment.
Can I pay with cash?
Payment methods depend on the individual expert. Many accept cash on arrival, while others may prefer digital transfers (QR PromptPay). You can clarify this in the chat before booking.
